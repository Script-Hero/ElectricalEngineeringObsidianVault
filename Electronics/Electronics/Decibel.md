We look at power changes due to frequency response in units of *decibels* because many systems are sensitive to a very wide range of power signals.

$$1\text{Bell}=\log_{10}(\frac{P_{in}}{P_{out{}}})$$
$$1\text{dB}=10\log_{10}(\frac{P_{in}}{P_{out{}}})$$
- $P$ is the Power of the input and the output of the signal

## Example
Since $V_{RMS}=\frac 1 {\sqrt{2}} V_{Peak}$, the power in a resistor due to sinusoidal voltage $v(t)=V_p\cos(\omega t)$ is $P=\frac{V_{RMS}}{R}=\frac{1}{2}\frac{V_p^2}{R}$
