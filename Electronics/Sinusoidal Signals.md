Any signal can be decomposed into its DC and AC components:
$$y_{AC}=y_{DC}+y_{ac}(t)$$
- $y_{DC}$ is the time invariant component
- $y_{ac}(t)$ is the time-dependent component
	- Lowercase variable letters


We express signals as $v(t)=A\cos(\omega t + \phi)\text{V}$
- $A$ is amplitude (height on the y-axis)
- $\omega$ is frequency (repetitions per second)
- $\phi$ is the phase (distance from the beginning of the sinusoidal to $t=0$)
