A representation of negative numbers in binary.

# Steps
1. Write the positive number in binary
2. Flip all the bits
3. Add 1

You can repeat this process to get back to the positive version of the number


## Example: Encoding decimal -5
1. 0000 0101 (*positive 5*)
2. 1111 1010 (*flipped bits*)
3. 1111 1011 (*add 1*)
The two's compliment representation of -5 is 1111 1011
#### Reverse reverse!
1. 0000 0100
2. 0000 0101 (add 1)
3. This is positive 5 in binary
