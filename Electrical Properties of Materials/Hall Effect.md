When the flow of electrons in solids is deflected by external magnetic field *due to the [[Lorentz Force]]*

Hall effect [[Voltage]]:
$$U_y=\frac{1}{eN_e}\cdot\frac{I_x}{t}\cdot B_z$$
- $e$ = charge of electron $1.6\cdot10^{-19}$ C
- $N_e=$ free electron density / concentration
- $I_x$ = *bias [[Current]]* (generated *bias [[Voltage]] $U_x$*)
- $t$ = thickness of [[Conductor]] along the direction of the magnetic field
- $B_z$ = external magnetic field (in T)

 When selecting a material, a good choice is something with a low number of electron holes that still conducts some amount of [[Current]]
 - [[Semiconductor]] = best
 - [[Insulator]]: $I_x=0$
 - Metal: Too high $N_e$
