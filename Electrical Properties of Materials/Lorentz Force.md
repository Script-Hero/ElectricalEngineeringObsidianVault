When an electron moves through a static magnetic field, the electron gets "pulled" (curved motion when the electron would otherwise go straight).
- Right hand rule
$$\vec{F}_\text{Lorentz}=q\cdot\vec v \times \vec B$$
- If $q$ is an electron then $\vec{F}_\text{Lorentz}=-e\cdot\vec v \times \vec B$
