Electrons repeatedly collide into much larger and "vibrating" atomic centers
- Erratic: stop, slow down, change direction, etc

The magnitude and frequency of collisions is affected by:
- [[Atomic Structure]] (material)
- crystal lattice arrangement (material)
- vibration of atomic centers (temperature)

# In the presence of an electric field
All electrons start to accelerate and drift in the direction of the field: there is a net [[Current]] flow in the same direction of the field 
- However, the acceleration can only last a short while between two collisions
	- stop-and-go process
- There is a *statistically average time interval and distance*
	- **Mean Free Time ($\tau$)**: Free moving time between two collisions
	- **Mean Free Path**: Free moving distance between 2 collisions
	- **Average Drift Velocity:** $V_d=a\tau$
