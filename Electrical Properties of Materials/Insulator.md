## NaCl
![[insulator_nacl.png]]
- All electrons tightly confined to their atomic masters (few free electrons)
- In this example (NaCl) charged Na ions and negatively charged Cl ions are directly bonded to each other through attractive *electrostatic* forces (*ionic bond*)
