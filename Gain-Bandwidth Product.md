GBP is used for fast calculations based on visual inspection of the op-amp [[Bode Plot]]

The gain-bandwidth product (GBP) of any given [[Electronics/Operational Amplifier|Operational Amplifier]] is constant after the first pole; the [[Transfer Function]] is: $$H(s)=\frac{A}{1+\frac{s}{\omega_{1}}=\frac A {1+\frac{j\omega}{\omega_1}}}$$
- If $\frac{\omega}{\omega_{1}}\gg1$ then $|{H(s)}|A \approx\frac{\omega_1}{\omega}$

For any frequency:
- $A_0\times\omega_0=A_1\times\omega_1=A_2\times\omega_2=K_\omega$
Or
- $A_{0}\times f_0=A_{1}\times f_{1}=A_{2}\times f_2=K_f$
