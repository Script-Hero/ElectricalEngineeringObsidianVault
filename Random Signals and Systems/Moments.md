#Chapter6 
Moments of a [[Random Variable]] $X$ are the generalization of [[Expected Value]] and [[Variance]].
$E[X^n]=\sum \limits_{x}x^np_{X}(x)$
- The 1st moment is [[Expected Value]]
- 2nd moment relates to [[Variance]], $Var[E] = E[X^2]-(E[X])^2$  
- 3rd moment relates to skewness (asymmetry)
- 4th moment relates to kurtosis (tail heaviness)
