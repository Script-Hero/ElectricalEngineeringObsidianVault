#Chapter8 

[[Continuous Random Variable]] with [[Probability Density Function]] $f_{X}=\lambda e^{-\lambda x}$, $x\geq 0$
- $\lambda$ characterizes the rate at which events occur
- Used to model lifetime of devices and systems, the time elapsed between specific occurrences

### Characteristics
- [[Expected Value]] $E[X]=\frac{1}{\lambda}$
- [[Variance]] $Var[X]=\frac{1}{\lambda^2}$

- For any $a>0$, $P(X\geq a)=e^{-\lambda a}$
