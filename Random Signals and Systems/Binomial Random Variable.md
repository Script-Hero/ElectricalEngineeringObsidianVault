#Chapter5 
A type of [[Random Variable]] that models the ***number of successes** in a fixed number series of independent [[Bernoulli Random Variable]] trials 
- Examples:
	- Number of defective products in a batch
	- Number of customers who actually make a purchase
	- Number of people responding to a survey
[[Probability Mass Function]] is $p_{X}(k)=P(X=k)=\begin{pmatrix}n\\ k\end{pmatrix}p^k(1-p)^{n-k}$
- where $\begin{pmatrix}n\\ k\end{pmatrix}$ is the "$n$ choose $k$" formula, which is $\frac{n!}{(n-k)!k!}$
