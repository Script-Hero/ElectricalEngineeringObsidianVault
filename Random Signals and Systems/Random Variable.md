#Chapter5

- A real-valued function of the experimental outcome
- Mapping of the sample space to an actual outcome / output value
- *Examples*
	- Sum of 2 die rolls
	- Amount of time needed to transmit a message
	- The second die roll raised to the 5th power
- *Types:*
	- [[Discrete Random Variable]]
		- [[Bernoulli Random Variable]]
		- [[Binomial Random Variable]]
		- [[Poisson Random Variable]]
		- [[Geometric Random Variable]]
	- [[Functions of Random Variables]]
	- [[Multiple Random Variables]]
	- [[Conditional Random Variable]]
	- [[Independent Random Variable]]
	- [[Continuous Random Variable]]
		- [[Uniform Random Variable]]
		- [[Exponential Random Variable]]
		- [[Gaussian Random Variable]]
	- [[Multiple Continuous Random Variables]]


### Characteristics of Random Variables include:
- [[Expected Value]]
- [[Variance]]
- [[Moments]]
- [[Affine Function]] 
- [[Linearity of Expectation]]
- [[Cumulative Distribution Function]]
- If $X$ is [[Discrete Random Variable]]
	- Has a [[Probability Mass Function]]
- If $X$ is [[Continuous Random Variable]]
	- Has a [[Probability Density Function]]
- [[Joint Probability Mass Function]]
