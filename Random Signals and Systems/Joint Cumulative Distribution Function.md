[[Cumulative Distribution Function]] for joint [[Multiple Continuous Random Variables]]
$$
F_{X,Y}(x,y)=\Pr(X\leq x,Y\leq y)=\int_{s=-\infty}^x\int_{t=-\infty}^yf_{X,Y}(x,y)dxdy
$$

### Computing [[Joint Probability Density Function]]
$$
f_{X,Y}=\frac{\delta^2F_{X,Y}}{\delta x \delta y}(x,y)
$$
### Properties 
If $X$ and $Y$ are [[Independent Random Variable]]s then $F_{X,Y}(x,y)=F_X(x)F_Y(y)$
