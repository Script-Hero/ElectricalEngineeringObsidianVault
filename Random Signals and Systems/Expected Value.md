Expected Value or Mean of a [[Probability Mass Function]] $X$ is $E[X]=\sum \limits_{x}x\cdot p_{X}(x)$
- Weighted average of possible values of $X$
- Can also be considered the "center of gravity" of the weighted outcomes

- $E[X+Y]=E[X] + E[Y]$
### Expected Value of different types of [[Random Variable]]:
- [[Bernoulli Random Variable]] $E[X]=p$
- [[Binomial Random Variable]] $E[X]=np$
- [[Poisson Random Variable]] $E[X] = \lambda$
- [[Geometric Random Variable]] $E[X]=\frac{1}{p}$
- [[Functions of Random Variables]] $E[Y]=\sum\limits_{x}g(x)p_{X}(x)$
	- Works out to $E[Y]=E[g(x)]$


[[Variance]] is the dispersion of $X$ around the mean
