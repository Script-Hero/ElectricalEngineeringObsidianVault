[[Continuous Random Variable]] with all intervals of a same length being equally probable. [[Probability Density Function]] is:

$$
f_{X}(x)=\begin{cases}
\frac{1}{b-a}&x\in[a,b]\\0,&\mbox{otherwise.}
\end{cases}
$$

### Characteristics
- [[Expected Value]] $E[X]=\frac{b+a}{2}$
- [[Variance]] $Var[X]=\frac{(b-a)^2}{12}$
