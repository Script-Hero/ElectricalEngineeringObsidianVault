#Chapter5 
A [[Random Variable]] that has *only 2 possible outcomes: $[0,1]$* 
- [[Probability Mass Function]] is represented as $p_{X}(x)=\begin{cases}1-p,&x=0\\ p,&x=1\end{cases}$
- where $p\in[0,1]$

Trials until success is [[Geometric Random Variable]]
Number of successes in $n$ trials is [[Binomial Random Variable]]
