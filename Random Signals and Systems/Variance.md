#Chapter6 
Measure of the dispersion of [[Random Variable]] $X$ around its mean or [[Expected Value]]
$$
Var[X]=E[(X-E[X])^2]
$$
- Always non-negative
- [[Standard Deviation]] is the square root of variance $\sigma(X)=\sqrt{ Var[X] }$

### In terms of [[Moments]]
- Variance can also be computed as $E[X^2]-(E[X])^2$

### Variance of Different [[Random Variable]]s
- [[Bernoulli Random Variable]] $Var[X]=p(1-p)$
- [[Poisson Random Variable]] $Var[X]=\lambda$
