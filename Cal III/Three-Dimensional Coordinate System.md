## Equations:

For more detail see [[Equations of Lines and Planes in 3D Space]]

In 2D we use $y=mx + b$

General equation of a plane_ $ax + by + cz = d$
Function in 3d space is f(x,y) = z

* A 2d equation ($y=mx+b$) can be extended into 3D plane without any modifications (0, 1, 1) and (0, 1, 2) both satisfy $y = 2x + 1$
	* Therefore, $y = mx+b$ gives a **plane** when in 3D space!
