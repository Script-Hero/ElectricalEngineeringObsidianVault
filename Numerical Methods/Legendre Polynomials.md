A sequence of orthogonal polynomials that are defined over the interval $[-1,1]$ and are orthogonal with respect to the weight function $w(x)=1$, meaning that
$$
\int\limits_{-1}^1P_m(x)P_n(x)dx=\begin{cases}0&\text{if }m\neq n\\\frac{2}{2n+1}&\text{if }m=n\end{cases}
$$
# Definition
The **Legendre polynomials** are generated with the recurrence relation:
- $P_0(x)=1$
- $P_1(x)=x$
- $P_{n+1}(x)=\frac{(2n+1)xP_{n}(x)-nP_{n-1}(x)}{(n+1)}$
