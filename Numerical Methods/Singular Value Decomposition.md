**Singular Value Decomposition**
1. Compute $A^TA$ and $AA^T$
2. Eigenvalues of $A^TA$ give the the squares of singular values $\sigma_i^2$
3. Eigenvectors of $A^TA$ give the columns of $V$
4. Eigenvectors of $AA^T$ give the columns of $U$ 
5. $\Sigma$ is a diagonal matrix containing the singular values
6. $A=U\Sigma V^T$
