Approximations of the solution of ordinary differential equations.


# Explicit Methods
- Compute the next value $y_{n+1}$ directly as a function of the [[Current]] step, using $t_n$ and $y_n$ 
### Types
 - [[Forward Euler Method]]
 - [[Explicit Runge-Kutta Methods]]


# Implicit Methods
- Requires solving an equation where $y_{n+1}$ appears on both sides 
- Often require iterative solvers 
### Types
- [[Backward Euler Method]]
- [[Trapezoid Rule]]
