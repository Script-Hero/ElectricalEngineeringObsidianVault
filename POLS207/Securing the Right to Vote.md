**Elimination of property qualifications**: happened by the early 1800s
**Fifteenth amendment**: In 1870, banned *race* discrimination
**Nineteenth Amendment**: In 1920, banned *gender* discrimination
**The "White Primary"**: Deemed unconstitutional in 1944
- Primary elections held in the South where only white people could vote

**Discrimination**: An ongoing problem even into the twentieth century
**Civil Rights Act of 1964**: Banned "Separate but Equal" (unequal standards)
**Twenty-Fourth Amendment**: 1964, banned poll taxes
**Voting Rights Act of 1965**: Largely eliminated discrimination in voting
**Twenty-Sixth Amendment**: 1971, lowered voting age to 18 years old
