- Like counties, municipal governments are creations of the state
- The Home-Rule Charter Amendments, 1912, enabled cities to adopt home-rule charters with a majority vote of qualified voters
	- These provide rules under which a city will operate and the form of government, and may grant the power to annex land

**Cities and towns less than 5,000 are chartered by general statute**
- These "general-law" cities and towns may act or organize themselves only as explicitly permitted by statutory law passed by the state legislature
	- For example, levy, assess, and collect taxes
- Cities and towns more than 5,00 can adopt charters
	- Home rule
