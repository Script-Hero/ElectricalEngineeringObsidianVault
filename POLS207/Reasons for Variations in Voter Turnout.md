**Socioeconomic explanations**: High education and / or high income means a person is more likely to vote
**Partisan competition-related explanations**: High turnout in swing states
**Media predicting winners early explanations**: Discourages later voters
**Legal and Procedural explanations**: 
- Differences in registration procedures
- Federally mandated "motor voter" registration law
	- Requires voters to register to vote when getting a driver's license
- **Differences in actual voting experience**: time, place, equipment, ballots, and poll workers
