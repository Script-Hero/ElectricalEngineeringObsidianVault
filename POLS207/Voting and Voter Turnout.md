- Voting is a common method of [[Political Participation]] and a right in a democracy
	- Yet many people do not vote
- **Voter Turnout** is the percentage of the voting age population or registered voters that cast ballots in an election
	- Turnout is *lowest* in local elections
- A "rational voter" is one who will only vote if the personal benefits outweigh the costs
- There are many [[Reasons for Variations in Voter Turnout]]
