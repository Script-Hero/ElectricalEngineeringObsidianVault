
[[Diff-Eq Homogenity Hiarchy]]

*Linear* differential equation is in the form:
	$a_0(t)y^{(n)}+a_1(t)y^{(n-1)}+...+a_n(t)y=g(t)$

## Linear differential equations can be Homogenous or nonhomogenous:
---

**Homogenous Differential Equations** are in terms of y and are equal to 0.

**Nonhomogenous Differential Equations** are set to anything that is not 0, including a function that includes $dx$.


---

**Ex:** $u_{xx}+ u_{yy}+ u_{x}+ 2u =0$
- PDE
- Order = 2 ($u_{xx}$ and $u_{yy}$)
- Linear
- Homogenous
