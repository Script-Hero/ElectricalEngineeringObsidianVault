
Consider a differential equation in the *normal form* $\frac{dy}{dt} = f(t,y)$

The derivative $y'$ represents slopes of the tangent lines on the graph $y(t)$. We can draw *line segments* on the $ty$ plane to create a *direction field* that illustrates the overall behavior of solutions to the equation.

Direction field of $y' = y-2$
![[Direction Field Example.png]]

Based on the direction field we can see what happens as $t\rightarrow\infty$.

Since the integral curves follow the lines of the slope field, we can see the behavior of each integral curve. When the curves of the slope field is flat then we can see the equilibrium solution.
