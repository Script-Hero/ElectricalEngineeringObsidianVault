
## Classification of Differential Equations 
- **Ordinary** differential equations *depend* on only one variable
	- **Partial** differential equations *depend* on *multiple* variables
	- **Systems** of differential equations are also possible, similarly to regular systems of equations
- **Order** is the highest level of differentiation in the equation. An *n*th Order differential equation has up to an *n*th derivative in it
- **Linear** equations are in the form $F(t,y,y',y^{(n)})=0$ where $F$ is a *linear* function
	- Linear equations are in the form $a_{0}ty^{n}+a_{1}ty^{n-1}+...=g(t)$ where $a_n$ is a function of *only $t$*. 
	- Nonlinear are when $F$ is *nonlinear*
