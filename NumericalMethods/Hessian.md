The transpose of the [[Jacobian]] of the gradient of the function $f$, that is: $\mathbf{H}(f(x))=J(\Delta f(x))^T$

The Hessian is computed as: 
$$
H_f=\begin{bmatrix} \frac{\delta^2 f}{\delta x_1^2}&\frac{\delta^2 f}{\delta x_1\delta x_2}&\dots & \frac{\delta f}{\delta x_1x_n}\\ \frac{\delta^2 f}{\delta x_1x_2}&\frac{\delta^2 f}{\delta x_2^2}&\dots & \frac{\delta f}{\delta x_2x_n}\\\dots&\dots&\dots&\dots\\ \frac{\delta^2 f}{\delta x_nx_1}&\frac{\delta^2 f}{\delta x_n\delta x_2}&\dots & \frac{\delta f}{\delta x_n^2}\end{bmatrix}
$$
