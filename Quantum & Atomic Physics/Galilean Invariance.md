**Definition: The laws of physics are the *same* in all *inertial reference frames***

*Additionally:*

-  a. That if newton's laws are valid in one reference frame, they are also valid in another reference frame moving at a constant velocity relative to the first system
-  b. the same as the Newtonian Principle of Relativity
-  c. that the Newton's laws are invariant in inertial reference frames
