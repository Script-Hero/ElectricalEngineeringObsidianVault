### 3) A clock in a spaceship is observed to run at a speed of only $\frac{3}{5}$ of a similar clock at rest on Earth. How fast is the spaceship moving (in terms of $c$)?
#### b. 4/5c
- The problem provides the [[Time Dilation]] *factor* of $\frac{3}{5}$, which is the *inverse* of the [[Lorentz Factor]] $\gamma$ , giving us $\gamma=\frac{5}{3}$. We can then plug this into $\gamma=\frac{1}{\sqrt{1-\frac{v^2}{c^2}}}$ 
#### NOT 9/10c, 3/5c, 2/5c, 1/5c

### 4) A spaceship of length 40m when at rest is observed to be 20m long when in motion. How fast is it moving? 
#### b. 0.86c 
- This is a [[Length Contraction]] problem, where we have to relate the length in motion $L'$ and the length at rest $L$ with the equation $L'=L\sqrt{1-\frac{v^2}{c^2}}$

### 13) In the Compton effect, which wavelength scatters through the largest angle?
#### c. All wavelengths scatter the same
- Compton Effect formula is $\Delta \lambda = \lambda_{e}(1-\cos\theta)$

	- $\theta$ is scattering angle
- Describes how photons scatter off an electron which causes a shift in the photon's wavelength 
- **The wavelength shift matters only on $\theta$, so the wavelength of the incoming photon doesn't matter.**
