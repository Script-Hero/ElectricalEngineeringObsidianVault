#Chapter8 

In heavier [[Many-Electron Atoms]] (with a bigger nucleus charge) spin-orbit interactions are stronger than spin-spin interactions or orbit-orbit interactions.  
- Each [[Orbital Angular Momentum Quantum Number]] combines with the corresponding [[Magnetic Spin Quantum Number]] creating an individual [[Total Angular Momentum Quantum Number]] $j_i$, then all these $j_i$s combine to create one large $J$
- Meaning $L+S=J$ *for each electron*
