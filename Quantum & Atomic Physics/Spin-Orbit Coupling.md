**Spin-Orbit Coupling** An effect of the interaction between the spins of the electron (see [[Magnetic Spin Quantum Number]]) and the orbital angular momentum (see [[Orbital Angular Momentum Quantum Number]]). This is for [[Hydrogen Atom]] single-electron atoms, and not [[Many-Electron Atoms]]. 

1. The dipole potential energy $V_{sl}=-\vec{\mu_s}\cdot\vec{B}_{\mbox{internal}}$
2. The spin magnetic moment $\propto -\vec{S}$
3. $\vec{B}_{\mbox{internal}}\propto\vec L$
- $\vec{B}_{\mbox{internal}}$ is the magnetic field due to the proton
- $V_{sl}\sim\vec S \cdot \vec L = SL\cos\alpha$
- where $\cos\alpha$ is the angle between $\vec S$ and $\vec L$
