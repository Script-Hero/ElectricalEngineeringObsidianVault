[[Quantum Number]] $m_s$ -- direction of electron's magnetic field relative to its central axis angle
## Lore
- Samuel Goudsmit and George Uhlenbach in Holland proposed that *the electron must have an intrinsic angular momentum* and therefore *an [[Intrinsic Magnetic Moment]]*
- Paul Ehrenfaust showed that the *surface of the spinning electron should be faster than the speed of light!*
- To explain this experimental data, Samuel Goudsmit and George Uhlenbach propose that *the electron must have an intrinsic spin number of* $s=\frac{1}{2}$ 
- This being added to the [[Quantum Number]] was done by Wolfgang Pauli (of the [[Pauli Exclusion Principle]])

## Fax
- Magnetic Spin [[Quantum Number]] has *only* 2 possible values: $m_s=\pm\frac{1}{2}$
	- Meaning an electron's spin will either be "up" or "down" and can never be spinning with its magnetic moment $\mu_s$ exactly along the $z$ axis
- **Intrinsic spin angular momentum vector** $|\vec S|=\sqrt{s(s+1)}\hbar=\sqrt{\frac{3}{4}}\hbar$
- **Magnetic Moment** is $\vec\mu_s=-(\frac{e}{m})\vec S$ or $-2\mu_B \frac{\vec{S}}{\hbar}$
	- The coefficient $\frac{\vec{S}}{\hbar}$ is $-2\mu_B$ as with $\vec{L}$ (see [[Orbital Angular Momentum Quantum Number]]) is a consequence of theory of relativity. 
#### The **Gyromagnetic Ratio** ($l$ or $s$)
If $g_l=1$ and $g_s=2$ then:
1. $\vec{\mu_l}=-\frac{g_l\mu_B\vec L}{\hbar}=-\frac{\mu_B \vec{L}}{\hbar}$
2. $\vec{\mu_s}=-2\frac{\mu_B\vec{S}}{\hbar}$
3. The $z$ component of $\vec{S}$ is $S_z=m_s \hbar=\pm\frac{\hbar}{2}$
4. In $l=0$ state, there is: 
	1. No splitting due to $\vec{\mu_s}$
	2. Yes space quantization due to *intrinsic spin*
5. Apply $m_l$ and the potential energy becomes $V_B=-\vec{\mu_s}\cdot\vec{B}=+\frac{e}{m}\vec S\cdot \vec B$
