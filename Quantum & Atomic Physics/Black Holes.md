**Schwarzschild Radius** $r_s=\frac{2GM}{c^2}$
- distance from center of black hole that light can't escape

**Hawking Radiation** particle + anti-particle pairs being made near the [[Event]] horizon, only one of the particles escapes the black hole -- decreases the total mass-energy of the black hole

**Gravitational Waves** When a charge accelerates it creates an *electromagnetic wave*, when mass accelerates it creates a *gravitational wave*
- difficult to detect, have to use big objects
- LIGO is a large michelson interferometer that detects changes in the lengths of its 4 perpendicular arms as a result of a passing gravitational wave
- Taylor + Hulse binary star system discovery that is losing energy because of their gravitational waves
