- Proton's intrinsic magnetic moment points in the same direction as its intrinsic spin angular moment (see [[Total Angular Momentum]])
- Measured in units of the nuclear magnetron $\mu_N=\frac{e\hbar}{2m_p}$
- much smaller than Bohr magnetron from [[Normal Zeeman Effect]]
- nonzero intrinsic magnetic moment means the atom has negative and positive charge components at different radii
- related to "nuclear magnetic resonance"
