- Designed to detect the presence of *Ether*, which was assumed to be the "medium" that light waves traveled through (in the same sense that sound waves *have to* travel through air)
- Found no detectable difference in the speed of light regardless of the direction of the motion relative to Earth's orbit

**Conclusion: the speed of light is constant in all inertial frames, *regardless of the observer's motion***


- This is where the [[Perpendicular Swimmers Problem]] comes from
