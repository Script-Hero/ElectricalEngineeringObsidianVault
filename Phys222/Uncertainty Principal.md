### Heisenberg Uncertainty Principle (Exam 2)
$\Delta x \Delta p \geq \frac{\hbar}{2}$
- the less uncertainty you have in length (location) the more uncertainty you have in the momentum
- this is an intrinsic physical property, no matter how good your measurement tools are you still can't beat this limit
