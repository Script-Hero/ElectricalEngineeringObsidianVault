#Chapter12 

Marie Curie!!!
- discovered polonium and radium
- simplest decay form is gamma ray, representing the nucleus changing from an excited state to lower energy state
- other forms of decay are $\alpha$ particles, $\beta$ particles, protons, neutrons, and [[Fission]]

Radioactive Decay: $N(t)=N_0 e^{-\lambda t}$ where $\lambda$ is a decay parameter
- Mean lifetime is $\frac{1}{\lambda}$

### Conservation
- When a nucleus decays, all conservation laws must be observed:
	- mass 
	- linear momentum
	- angular momentum
	- electric charge
	- nucleons
		- aka mass number

### Alpha Decay
- Decay of radioactive nucleus releasing alpha particle and splitting atom into 2 "chunks"
- Alpha particle is made up of 2 protons and 2 neutrons
- atomic number decreases by 2
- mass number decreases by 4

### Beta Decay
$n\rightarrow p+\beta^{-}$
- neutrino (invented by Wolfgang Pauli) carries away excess energy caused by spin imbalance of $p$ spin of 1/2 and Beta^- spin of 1 cannot cause a spin of 0 (conservation)
- neutrino has little mass no charge and not impacted by the strong force

### Gamma Decay
If the change goes from less excited state --> *more* excited state, then $Q$ for the transition is $Q=Q_0 - E_x$
