- Time increasing for *outside observer* when you are traveling at relativistic speeds (*near $c$*). A clock on a spaceship will runs slower than a clock at rest (aka on Earth)


The [[Lorentz Factor]] $\gamma$ is equal to $\frac{t}{t'}$
- Where $t$ is the duration of time for the observer who is *at rest*
- $t'$ is the duration of time for an observer *in motion on the spaceship!*



$t'=t\sqrt{1-\frac{v^2}{c^2}}$
- $t'$  is the time for the observer in motion
