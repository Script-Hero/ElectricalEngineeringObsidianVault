### Strong Nuclear Force
- quickly --> 0 when particles separate
- spin dependent
- "aligned for the bound state of the duetron" but no bound state when spins are anti-aligned 
- independent of their charge

### Stability

- typically even Z and even N 
- for A<= 40, most stability is when N = Z, when A > 40, then most stability when N > Z
