#Chapter12 

We use the [[Fermi Distribution]] to describe the charge density as a function of radial distance $r$.
![[Pasted image 20241126142719.png]]
- $\rho_0$ is charge density at nucleus
- $R$ is "nuclear radius" distance at which density drops significantly
- $a$ is "diffuseness" parameter referring to how quickly density falls near the nucleus edge
- **Works best for *light* nuclei**
