#Chapter7 

### Allowed Transitions
- Electrons absorbing or emitting photons to change state when change in [[Orbital Angular Momentum Quantum Number]] $\Delta l = \pm 1$
	- [[Principal Quantum Number]] $\Delta n =\mbox{anything}$
	- [[Magnetic Quantum Number]] $\Delta m_l=0,\pm1$
	- [[Total Angular Momentum Quantum Number]] $\Delta j=0,\pm1$

### Forbidden Transitions
- Other transitions are "possible" but occur with much smaller probabilities than when $\Delta l \neq \pm 1$
