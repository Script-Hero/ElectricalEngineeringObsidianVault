#Chapter8 

*Version* of the [[Normal Zeeman Effect]] with **more than 3 spectral line emissions**.

- The interaction that splits the energy levels in an external magnetic field $\vec{B}_\mbox{ext}$ is caused by $\vec\mu \cdot \vec B$ interaction. 
- The magnetic moment depends on:
	1. orbital magnetic moment of $\vec\mu_l$
	2. spin magnetic moment $\vec\mu_s$
- No more $2J+1$ degeneracy for a given state due to the effect of $\vec{B}_\mbox{ext}$
- If $\vec{B}_\mbox{ext}$ is small compared to the internal magnetic field, then $\vec L$ and $\vec S$ precess about $\vec J$ and $\vec J$ precesses *slowly* about $\vec B_\mbox{ext}$

**Total magnetic moment** is $\vec\mu = \vec\mu_l + \vec\mu_s = -\frac{e}{2m}(\vec J + \vec S)$
- $V=\frac{e\hbar B_\mbox{ext}}{2m}gm_J=\mu_BB_\mbox{ext}gm_J$
	- where $\mu_B$ is the Bohr magnetron and
	- ![[Pasted image 20241126064256.png]]
		- is called the "Lande g factor"
