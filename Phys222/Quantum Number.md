4. $m_s$ -- [[Magnetic Spin Quantum Number]]
5. $j$ -- [[Total Angular Momentum Quantum Number]]

### Restrictions on Quantum Numbers

Following the [[Wave Function Boundary Conditions]] creates the restrictions on the quantum numbers $l$ and $m$:
#### Numerical Restrictions
- $n=1,2,3,4,\dots$
- $l=0,1,2,3,\dots,n-1$
- $m_l=-l, -l+1, \dots, -2, -1, 0, 1, 2, \dots, l-1, l$
#### Relational Restrictions
- $n>0$
- $|m_l|\leq l$
- $l < n$
