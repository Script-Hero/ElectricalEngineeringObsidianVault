[[Quantum Number]] $n$, resulting from $E_n=\frac{-\mu}{2}(\frac{e^2}{4\pi\epsilon_0\hbar})\frac{1}{n^2}=-\frac{E_0}{n^2}$ -- **primarily determines electron's distance from nucleus**

- Used to describe *energy level* of the quantum object
- This creates the quantized nature of the Principal Quantum Number
- Negative sign indicates that the electron and proton are bound together

Can be any integer greater than 0.

Relates to which shell in the atom the electron is located in.
