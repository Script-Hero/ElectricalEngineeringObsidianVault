**An observer moving at relativistic speeds will measure objects as *shorter* than observers who are at rest**

$L'=L\sqrt{1-\frac{v^2}{c^2}}$
- $L'$ is the length according to the observer *in motion*
- $L$ is length according to the observer *at rest*
- $v$ is how fast that object is moving relative to the observer *at rest*
