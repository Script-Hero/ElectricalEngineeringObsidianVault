## Division of Power
- Supremacy Clause: Article VI of the Constitution states the following are supreme over the state constitutions:
	- The U.S. Constitution
	- Laws of Congress
		- Treaties
	- Yet national government cannot usurp state powers
		- Tenth Amendment

## Establishing National Supremacy
- Implied and enumerated powers
	- *McCulloch v. Maryland (1819)*
- Commerce Powers
	- *Gibbons v. Ogden (1824)*
- The Civil War
- The Struggle for Racial Equality
	- *Brown v. Board of Education (1954)*

## States' Obligations to Each Other
- Full Faith and Credit
	- Each state must recognize official documents and judgements rendered by other states
	- Article IV, Section I of the Constitution
- Privileges and Immunities: Citizens of each state have privileges of citizens of other states
	- Article IV, Section 2 of the Constitution
- Extradition
	- States must return a person charged with a crime in another state back to that state for punishment
