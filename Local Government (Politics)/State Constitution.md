- Most common method
- Amendment first passed by the legistlature
- Then put to the voters in a referendum

## Popular Initiative
- Citizen bypass legislature for a direct vote
- Must obtain requisite number of petition signatures

### Constitutional Revision Commissions

# Texas Constitution
## Notoriously poor document
- Outdated
- Over amended (517 in Texas, US constitution only has 27 amendments)
- Micromanages (opposite of "broadly constructed laws")
- Easy to amend, difficult to overhaul
	- Tack on laws easily
	- Scrapping laws and starting new laws is very hard; legislative time crunch


## Constitutional History
- Not really on the exam lol
- 7 different constitutions in Texas
- Typically changes because of changes on the national level (independence, joining union, seceding, joining again, etc)
