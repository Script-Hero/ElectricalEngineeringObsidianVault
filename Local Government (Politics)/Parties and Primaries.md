- Filing
- Endorsements
- Closed and Open Primaries
	- *Closed* only registered party members can vote in their party's primary
	- *Open* any registered voter can vote in any **one** party's primary (but not both)
- Runoff Primaries
	- When no candidate gets more than 50% of the vote in a primary election
	- Top 2 candidates face off in runoff election
- Conventions
- Presidential Primaries and Caucuses 
	- Presidential primary is like a statewide election / regular vote
	- Caucus isn't used often any more but voters physically group up and discuss, sometimes in multiple rounds, takes a while
