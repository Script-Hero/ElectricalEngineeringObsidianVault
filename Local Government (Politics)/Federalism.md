## Why is Federalism So Important?
- Decentralizes our politics
	- Creates more opportunities to participate
- Decentralizes our policies
	- Federal and state governments handle different problems
		- For example, states regulate drinking age, marriage, and speed limits
	- States can solve the same problem in different ways and tend to be policy innovators
- Many levels of government
- Increases access to government and allows local problems to be solved locally
