## Political Party Structure: Key Terms

### State Party Organizations
These are the official branches of a political party operating within each U.S. state. They help coordinate statewide election efforts, support candidates, raise funds, and communicate the party’s platform to voters at the state level.

---

### State Laws Govern Parties
Refers to the fact that political parties are subject to laws passed by state governments. These laws regulate how parties operate, how primaries are conducted, campaign finance, ballot access, and party registration.

---

### State Committees
These are governing bodies of a state party organization, typically made up of party leaders and elected members from across the state. They help set party strategy, approve rules, and oversee operations like nominating candidates and organizing conventions.

---

### State Party Chairpersons
The top officials of state party organizations. They lead the state committee, act as the public face of the party in the state, coordinate campaign strategies, and manage fundraising and outreach efforts.

---

### County Committees
These are local-level party committees that operate at the county (or parish) level. They help organize volunteers, run local events, support candidates in local elections, and serve as a link between the community and the state party.

---

### Local Party Organizations
These operate at the most grassroots level—towns, cities, wards, or precincts. They are responsible for voter outreach, distributing materials, canvassing, and turning out the vote during elections. They often consist of local volunteers and party activists.
