How policy comes into being and evolves over time


## The Cycle:
1. **People**
	1. Issues
	2. Concerns
	3. Problems
2. **Linkage Institutions**
	1. Public Opinion
	2. Media
	3. Political Parties
	4. Interest Groups
	5. Elections
3. **Policymaking Institutions**
	1. State Legislature
	2. Executive and Bureaucracy
	3. Judiciary
4. **Policies**
	1. Fiscal
	2. Education
	3. Social Services
5. **Policy [[Implementation]]**
	1. Requires feedback, so it loops back to the **People**
