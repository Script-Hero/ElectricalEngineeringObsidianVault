When individuals directly vote on things, rather than using representatives to do their voting for them. [[Direct vs Representative Democracy]]

Developed in states and communities following a strong populist movement. The populist and progressive reform movement:
- introduced a range of devices to bypass political institutions and encourage direct participation
- Supported women's suffrage, civil service, and restrictive immigration laws
- Were responsible for adoption of the *3 forms of direct democracy*
# Three Forms
1. **Initiative**: a percentage of voters using a petition may have a law or amendment placed on the ballet *without legislative involvement*
2. **Referendum**: electorate must approve legislative decisions before they become law
3. **Recall**: allows voters to remove an elected official before the end of their term
	- When voting in recall elections, you must answer 2 questions: if you want them to be recalled, and who you would like to replace them if the recall is successful. You answer both questions at the same time.
