- Political participation is the *essence and definition of democracy*
- Political participation includes:
	- voting
	- running for office
	- participating in marches or demonstrations
	- giving money or time to efforts
	- attending rallies or events
	- writing letters or emails
	- wearing a button
	- discussing issues with friends
	- belonging to an organization
- *Sustained political participation* refers to: consistent voting in presidential and nonpresidential elections
