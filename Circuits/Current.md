- Describes the movement or flow of electron charge
- Electrons go from one atom to another, pushing that atom's electrons forward, like a "marble track", but individual electrons don't go very far.
- $i=\frac{dq}{dt}(A)$
	- Current is the rate of change of charge times the cross section area of the wire 
- "Conventional Current" is from positive terminal to negative terminal, but "electron current" (reality) is from the negative terminal to the positive terminal, because electrons are negatively charged
- Current can be "Direct Current" (DC) or "Alternating Current" (AC)
