Using the theory from [[Thevenin and Norton Equivalence]] you can model any active circuit (a circuit with at least one independent power source) as a Thevenin voltage source, Thevenin resistance, and then you can model the passive circuit (no or only dependent power sources) as a load resistor. 

Derived from Ohm's Law

$$I_L=\frac{V_{TH}}{R_{TH}+R_L}$$
$$V_L=\frac{V_{TH}R_L}{R_{TH}+R_L}$$


## **Maximum Current Transfer:** $R_L\ll R_{TH}$
## **Maximum Voltage Transfer:** $R_L\gg R_{TH}$
## **Power:**  $P_L=I_LV_L=\frac{V_{TH}^2R_L}{(R_{TH}+R_L)^2}$
## **Maximum Power:** $R_L=R_{TH}$ or equivalently $\frac{V_{TH}^2}{4R_L}$
