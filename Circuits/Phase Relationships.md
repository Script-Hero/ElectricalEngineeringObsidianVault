Different circuit elements have different relationships between the *phase* of [[Voltage]] and [[Current]]. 

1. **Resistor** [[Voltage]] Phase = [[Current]] Phase
	1. $\pmb V = R \pmb I$
2. **Inductor** [[Voltage]] phase = [[Current]] Phase + $90\degree$
	1. $\pmb V = j\omega L \pmb I$
3. **Capacitor** [[Voltage]] phase = [[Current]] Phase - $90 \degree$
	1. $\pmb V = \frac{1}{j\omega C}\pmb I=-j\frac 1 {\omega C}$
