## General Steps
1. Use [[KVL]] or [[KCL]] to find a suitable [[Second Order Differential Equations]] to describe the circuit 
	1. [[Finding Second Order Circuit Equation]]
2. Use the physical initial state of the the circuit to determine the *initial conditions* for the differential equation
	1. [[Finding Second Order Circuit Initial Conditions]]
3. Solve the differential equation with the initial conditions
	1. [[Damping]]
4. If necessary, translate the quantity found in steps 1-3 to other related voltages, currents, energies, or powers
