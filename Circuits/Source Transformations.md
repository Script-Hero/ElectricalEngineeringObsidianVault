We can transform a series combination of a [[Voltage]] source to an equivalent parallel combination of a [[Current]] source and resistor (see [[Power Sources]] and [[Equivalent Resistance]])

![[source_transformation_annotated.png]]
- This equivalency is with respect to any load across terminals $a$ and $b$.

**These [[Circuits]] are equivalent if $V_s=I_s\cdot R$**
