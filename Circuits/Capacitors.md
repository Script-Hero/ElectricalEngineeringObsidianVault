Passive device.

$$i=C\frac{dv}{dt}$$
- [[Current]] equals capacitance times the rate of change of [[Voltage]] through the capacitor
- When a circuit reaches a steady state, a capacitor acts like an *open circuit*
- The *[[Voltage]]* across a capacitor *cannot change instantaneously*, otherwise creating infinite [[Current]]
$$v(t)=v(t_o)+\frac{1}{C}\int\limits_{t_o}^ti(u)du$$
- The *[[Power]]* of a capacitor is $p(t)=\frac C 2 \frac d {dt}(v^2(t))$
- The *energy* of a capacitor is $w(t)=\frac C 2 v^2 (t)$
- Capacitors combine *in series*: $\frac 1 {C_{eq}} = \frac 1 {C_1} \frac 1 {C_2}$
- Capacitors combine *in parallel*: $C_{eq} = C_1 + C_2$
