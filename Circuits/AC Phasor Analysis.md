All techniques for analyzing resistive [[Circuits]] apply to RLC [[Circuits]] when we use [[Phasors]] and [[Complex Impedance]]

1. [[KVL]]: $v_1(t)+v_2(t)+\dots+v_n(t)=0\leftrightarrow\pmb V_1 + \pmb V_2 +\dots + \pmb V_n=0$
2. [[KCL]]: $i_1(t)+i_2(t)+\dots+i_n(t)=0\leftrightarrow\pmb I_1 + \pmb I_2 +\dots + \pmb I_n=0$
3. Series combination of impedances: $Z_\text{eq}=Z_1+Z_2+\dots+Z_n$
4. Parallel combination of impedances: $\frac 1 {Z_\text{eq}} = \frac 1 {Z_1} + \frac 1 {Z_2}+\dots+\frac 1 {Z_n}$
5. [[Voltage]] & [[Current]] dividers, $\Delta$-Y transformations, [[Source Transformations]], Thevenin / Norton equivalents, node-[[Voltage]], mesh-[[Current]], etc
