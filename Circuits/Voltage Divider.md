If a [[Voltage]] is applied across a **series resistance combination** then the [[Voltage]] is divided across the resistors *proportionally to their individual resistances*
- **Higher [[Voltage]] goes to the *larger* resistor**

![[voltage_divider_circuit_example.png]]
- The [[Voltage]] $V_1=IR_1=V_{s}\frac{R_1}{R_1 + R_2}$
- The [[Voltage]] $V_2=IR_2=V_s\frac{R_2}{R_1 + R_2}$
- $R_1$ and $R_2$ share the same [[Current]] because they are in series
