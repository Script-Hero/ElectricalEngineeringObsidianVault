- Associated with the displacement / concentration of electron charge
- Electric potential, imagine water flowing down a hill
$$
V_{ab}=\frac{dw}{dq}
$$
- Voltage from point $a$ to $b$ is the change in energy (Joules, $dw$) over the change in charge $dq$
- We define a "ground" as the point in the circuit with no voltage and define all voltages in the rest of the circuit relative to that ground
