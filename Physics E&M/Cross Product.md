
- A measure of how *perpendicular* 2 vectors are
- Called vector product
- Only defined in 3D space
- Direction is defined by right hand rule

---

$|\vec{A} \times \vec{B}| = AB\sin\theta$  $\Rightarrow$ find direction by right hand rule

$\vec{A} \times \vec{B} = (A_{y}-B_{z})i_{x} + (A_{z}-B_{x})i_{y} + (A_{x}-B_{y})i_{z}$

$\vec{A} \times \vec{B} = \begin{bmatrix}  i_{x} & i_{y} & i_{z}\\  A_{x} & A_{y} & A_{z} \\  B_{x} & B_{y} & B_{z}\end{bmatrix}$

---

- Cross product is 0 for parallel or anti-parallel vectors
- $\vec{A} \times \vec{B} = -\vec{A} \times \vec{B}$
