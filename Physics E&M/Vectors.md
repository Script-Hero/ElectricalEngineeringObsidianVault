#Phys-EM

Vector addition:
	- If cartesian just add components
	- If polar:
		- Add components in polar *only if they're at the same point*
		- otherwise convert the polar coordinates to cartesian, add in cartesian, then convert back to polar

---

- Radial direction for polar coordinates goes out from the origin (in a 2D sense, **not out of the page**)
- Tangential direction is perpendicular from the radial direction **out of the page**
	- Traditionally we see use clockwise as the positive tangential direction
	- ***BE REALLY CAREFUL YOU'RE CHOOSING THE CORRECT TANGENTIAL DIRECTION***

---

Use trig to get conversion between coordinate systems:
$V^{2}= V_{x}^{2}+ V_{y}^{2}= V_{r}^{2} + V_{\theta}^2$
