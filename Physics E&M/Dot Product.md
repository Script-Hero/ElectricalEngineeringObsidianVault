
A type of vector [[Multiplication]]
- A measure of how much 2 [[Vectors]] point in the same direction
- AKA "inner product" or "scalar product"

$\vec{A} \cdot \vec{B} = AB\cos\theta = A_{x}B_{x} + A_{y}B_{y}$

If:
- $\vec{A}$ and $\vec{B}$ are perpendicular --> dot product is 0
-  $\vec{A}$ and $\vec{B}$ are parallel --> dot product is $AB$
-  $\vec{A}$ and $\vec{B}$ are antiparallel --> dot product is -$AB$


Line integral:
$\int_{C}\vec{F}(\vec{r}) \cdot d\vec{r}$
