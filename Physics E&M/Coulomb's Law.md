#Phys-EM 

$\vec{F_{21}}(\vec{r})=\frac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0}\frac{q_{1}q_{2}}{r^2}\hat{r}_{2 1}$
Where $\vec{F_{21}}$ denotes the force from charge 1 upon charge 2
- Note that $\frac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0}$ is a constant $k=8.99×10^{9}N \frac{m^2}{C^2}$
	- So you can think of it like $F=\frac{q_{1}q_{2}}{r^2}$
Sometimes charge is distributed instead of a point. See [[Distributed Charge]]
