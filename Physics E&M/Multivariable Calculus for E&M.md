
Partial derivative = $\frac{\delta f(x,y,z)}{\delta x} = \lim_{\Delta x \to 0} \frac{f(x + \Delta x, y, z) - f(x,y,z)}{\Delta x}$ 

Del "operator" : $\vec{\nabla} = \frac{\delta}{\delta_{x}}i_{x}+\frac{\delta}{\delta_{y}}i_{y} + \frac{\delta}{\delta_{z}}i_{z}$
- Finds derivative in spaces w arbitrary number of dimensions
- Meaningless on its own but used to find things like gradient, curl, divergence, etc

Gradient:  $\frac{\delta f}{\delta_{x}}i_{x}+\frac{\delta f}{\delta_{y}}i_{y} + \frac{\delta f}{\delta_{z}}i_{z}$
* Multivariable derivative, which is calculated by using the del operator

Divergence: $\vec{\nabla} \cdot \vec{F} = \frac{\delta}{\delta_{x}}F_{x}+\frac{\delta}{\delta_{y}}F_{y} + \frac{\delta}{\delta_{z}}F_{z}$
- Scalar value that represents how much the strength of a vector varies
- Measure of how much a vector expands and contracts
- Represents flux
