
Change in kinetic energy is the same as the total work done
$\Delta K = W_{tot}$

Potential energy ($U$) = $-\delta \vec{F}$ $\Rightarrow$ smart to break into the force's x and y components by taking partial derivates

Work = $\int^{\vec{r_{2}}}_{\vec{r_{1}}} F(x)$ where $F(x)$ is the function of a *conservative* force

Mechanical energy = $K + U$ = Kinetic energy + potential energy

If no external forces, both momentum and energy are conserved
