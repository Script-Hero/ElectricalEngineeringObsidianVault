#em-ch-3
**Flux** is the amount of [[Electric Field]] flowing through a particular area.

*This is used for [[Electric Fields]] stored **inside** of a surface*

$\phi$ is the flux of an [[Electric Field]], where $d\vec{S}$ is an infinitesimally small surface area.

$d\phi=\vec{E}\cdot d\vec{S}$
$\phi=\oint\vec{E}\cdot d\vec{S} = \frac{Q}{\epsilon_0}$

For a uniform field:
$\phi=\vec{E}\cdot\vec{S}$
- this is a [[Dot Product]]

In general, the net electric flux, $\phi$ is equal to the amount of charge $Q$ *enclosed by the surface* divided by the constant of electric permittivity $\epsilon_0$.
