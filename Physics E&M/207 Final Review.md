# 1
Coulomb's Law $d\vec{F}=\frac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_{0}}\frac{q_{0}dq}{r^{2}}\hat{r}$

Types of problems:
- a bunch of point charges (electrostatics)
- finding $E$ field from distributed charge

## Electric and Magnetic Flux
- Flux $\phi$ is amount of electric field passing through an *area*
- For a nonuniform field or nonflat area we use $dS$
	- use right hand rule to relate $dS$ and $dr$ when calculating magnetic flux
