EMFs are produced by:
1. A time varying $B$ field
2. an area whose size is varying
3. a time varying angle between $\vec{B}$ and $d\vec{S}$
4. any combination of the above

**Lenz's Law**
$\oint\vec{E}\cdot d\vec{r}=-\frac{d\phi_{B}}{dt}$

Mutual inductance $\phi_{B}=Mi$ where $M$ is mutual inductance
- usually determined experimentally, only calculated for simple cases

**Self-Inductance** 
$\phi_{B}=Li$
- Where $L$ is self-inductance
$\frac{d\phi_{B}}{dt}=L\frac{di}{dt}$

For a solenoid (straight coil) $B=\mu_{0}\frac{N}{l}i$
- -$L=\mu_{0}N^{2}\pi \frac{r^{2}}{l}$

**Summary**
$\oint \vec{E}\cdot d\vec{r}=-\frac{d}{dt}\int\vec{B}_{Total}\cdot d\vec{S}$
$\phi_{Self}=\int\vec{B}_{Self}\cdot d\vec{S}=Li$
