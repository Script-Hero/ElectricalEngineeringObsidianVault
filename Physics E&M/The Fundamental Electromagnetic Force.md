
4 Fundamental forces in the universe:
- Strong
- Weak
- Gravity
- **Electromagnetic**

Everything in the universe if made of charged fundamental particles
- Most objects have neutral electric charge
- [[Conductors]] allow electric charges to move
- Insulators prevent the movement of charge

Electric charges are only observed in integer multiples of a single electron's charge:
* In other [[words]], the only way an object receives charge is from the number of electrons it has 

* Net electric charge is conserved in an isolated system
	* But the number of charged *particles* may not be
		* That means the charge of one particle can be transferred to another
