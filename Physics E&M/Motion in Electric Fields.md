
Force on a charge *from* an [[Electric Field]] $\vec{F}=q\vec{E}$
- Comes from $\vec{E}=\lim_{q\to0}\frac{\vec{F}}{q_0}$

We can use this to get beams of charged particles.

$F=ma$ $\equiv$ $qE=ma$ $\equiv$ $a=\frac{q}{m}E$

If we have constant motion then $v=\int\frac{\epsilon}{m}=\frac{q}{m}Et + v(0)$ (note that $v(0)=0$)

$W = \int^{d}_{0}\vec{F}\cdot d\vec{r} = \int^{d}_{0}qEdx$

$W = \Delta E = \frac{1}{2}ma^{2}$
