To simplify analysis of balanced [[3 Phase Power]] systems, all $\Delta$-Connections are replaced by the equivalent Y-Connection.
- See [[Delta Y Equivalent Circuits]]

## Process
1. For $\Delta$-connected **loads (phases)**: $Z_Y=\frac{Z_\Delta}{3}$
2. For $\Delta$-connected **sources (lines)**: $Z_{LN,Y}=\frac{V_{LL,\Delta}}{\sqrt 3\angle30\degree}$
