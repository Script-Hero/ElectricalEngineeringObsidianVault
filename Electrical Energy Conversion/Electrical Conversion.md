Modern energy conversion is used for changing:
- [[Voltage]] level
- Frequency
- Waveshape 
- Number of Phases

Both sources and load sides can be either AC or DC
- [[Rectifier]]: from AC to DC
- [[Inverter]]: from DC to AC
- Converter / Transformer: [[DC-DC Converters]] / from AC to AC
