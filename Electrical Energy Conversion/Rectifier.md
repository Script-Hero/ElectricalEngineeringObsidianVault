For AC to DC [[Electrical Conversion]]
- Mostly used for low-[[Power]] applications
	- Because the average [[Current]] in the supply is *not* zero
	- This nonzero average [[Current]] may cause problems in transformer

## Need For Diodes
- We cannot change frequency ($60\text{Hz}\rightarrow0\text{Hz}$) with linear circuit elements
	- Magnitude and phase yes, but not frequency
- Need for non-linear elements, and one good option is [[Diode]]s
	- Turn on condition: $V_d>0$
	- Turn off condition: $I_\text{forward}\leq0$
	- Uncontrollable [[Electronic Switches]]
		- Circuit governs its on and off state
