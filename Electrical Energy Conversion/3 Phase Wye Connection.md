## Line-Neutral Connection ($V_{LN}$)
- $V_{an}=|V|\angle \alpha\degree$
- $V_{bn}=|V|\angle (\alpha-120)\degree$
- $V_{cn}=|V|\angle(\alpha+120)\degree$

## Line-Line Volage ($V_{LL}$) using KVL
- $V_{ab}=V_{an}-V_{bn}=\sqrt 3 V_{an}\angle 30\degree$
- $V_{bc}=V_{bn}-V_{cn}=\sqrt 3 V_{bn}\angle 30\degree$
- $V_{ca}=V_{cn}-V_{an}=\sqrt 3 V_{cn}\angle 30\degree$
- **In General:** $V_{LL}=\sqrt 3V_{LN}\angle30\degree$

**[[Voltage]] is given as line-line [[Voltage]] by convention.**
