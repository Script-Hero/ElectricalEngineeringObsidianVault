# Advantages over [[Half-Wave Rectifier]]
- Average [[Current]] in the AC source is zero
	- Avoids problems associated with nonzero average source [[Current]] (esp. in transformers)
- Output has less ripple
- [[Power]] utilization increased ([[Power]] is drawn during positive and negative parts of the AC wave)
	- Therefore achieves higher performance
