## Phase Current ($I_\text{Phase}$)
- $I_{ab}=|I|\angle\alpha\degree$
- $I_{bc}=|I|\angle(\alpha-120)\degree$
- $I_{ca}=|I|\angle(\alpha+120)\degree$

## Line Current ($I_\text{Line}$) using KCL
- $I_a=I_{ab}-I_{ca}=\sqrt 3I_{ab}\angle-30\degree$
- $I_b=I_{bc}-I_{ab}=\sqrt 3I_{bc}\angle-30\degree$
- $I_c=I_{ca}-I_{bc}=\sqrt 3I_{ca}\angle-30\degree$
- **In general:** $\sqrt 3 I_\text{Phase}\angle-30\degree$

**[[Current]] is given as line [[Current]] by convention.**
