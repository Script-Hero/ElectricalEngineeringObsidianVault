**Covariance** is the measure of how much two [[Random Variable]]s change together.
- If the **covariance** is positive, it means that when one variable increases the other *tends to* as well
- If the **covariance** is negative, it means that when one variable increases the other *tends to* decrease

$$
\text{Cov}(X,Y)=E[(X-E[X])\cdot(Y-E[Y])]
$$
