A **random variable** is a real-valued function of the outcome of an experiment involving randomness.

If the **experiment** is rolling two die, some possible random variables are:
- The sum of the rolls
- The difference of the rolls
- The maximum of the rolls
- The value of the first roll


A **discrete** random variable can take on at most a *countably infinite* number of possible values.

A **continuous** random variable has an *uncountable* amount of possible values.


## Relationship to Events
When we set a random variable equal to a value, we get an [[Event]], and all results about events and their [[Probability]]s apply.
