The **joint [[Random Signals and Systems/Probability Mass Function|Probability Mass Function]]** between 2 discrete [[Random Variable]]s $X$ and $Y$ is $P_{X,Y}(x,y)=P(X=x,Y=y)$ where $\sum\limits_{x}\sum\limits_{y}p_{x,y}(x,y)=1$
- **Marginal [[Probability]]** is how $p_X(x)$ is related to $p_{X,Y}(x,y)$
