Extension of [[Linear Regression]] by using a polynomial of higher degree. [[Linear Regression]] is the $\text{degree}=1$ case of polynomial regression.

$$y\approx w_0 + w_1 \cdot x_1 + w_2 \cdot x_2 + w_3 \cdot x_1^2 + w_4\cdot x_2^2$$
- Is one example of polynomial regression, but not the *only* example *even for the $\text{degree}=2$ case*
	- There are other possible degree 2 polynomial regression equations
