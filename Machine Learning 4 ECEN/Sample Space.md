Probability is defined in terms of some **experiment**:
- Experiment of tossing a coin
- Experiment of rolling a die twice

A **sample space** $\Omega$ is the *set of all possible outcomes of an experiment*
- Experiment of tossing a coin: $\Omega=\{H,T\}$
- Experiment of rolling a die twice: $\Omega=\{ (1,1),(1,2),\dots,(6,6) \}$
